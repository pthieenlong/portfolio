## Overview

A small game played in the phone browser. You inherit your late grandmother's village grocery store and serve customers one by one before the day ends, while a real-estate company tries to buy out the village before Tết. Dialogue is written in the Southern Vietnamese (miền Tây) dialect.

This is a for-fun project, currently at **v0.3**.

## How it plays

- A day lasts about 5 minutes. Customers arrive one at a time with a patience bar.
- Customers say what they need in dialogue; you tap items on the shelf into a tray and hand them over.
- Wrong items cost patience; running out of stock sends them home.
- Some customers tell a story after a successful sale and offer 3 replies.

## Architecture

- **`engine/`**: game rules as pure functions (`resolveSale`, `tick`, `restock`, `summarizeDay`) with no React dependency, covered by Vitest.
- **`content/`**: days, items and random visits defined in JSON. Adding a scenario means adding a file, not code.
- **`controllers/`**: a `GameController` owns state and the clock; React reads it through a `useStore` hook.
- **`ui/`**: screens and components only.

Every rule and balancing decision is documented in a `DECISIONS.md` that records *who* made each call, which keeps scope honest on a hobby project.
